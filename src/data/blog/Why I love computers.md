## How did I get into computers?
I got into computers at a young age, about around the age of 4 or 5 and I have been fascinated with them ever since, when I was young we had this Windows XP computer and at the time it was great. I've always been fascinated with how computers worked and functioned because we went from having a 'computer' that was the size of a room to a computer that can sit on a desk which is 100x more powerful than that.
