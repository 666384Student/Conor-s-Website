## Before we start

The past four years has been a long learning journey from learning new things, making new friends and whatnot. And I am still learning new things every day!

## Okay now onto the actual experience

Four years ago I started my level 2 course in computing and this was a huge change from being in secondary school (You actually get treated like a human being at college!), of course I was nervous to all ends for my first day at college but I quickly grew fond of being at college because you can actually call your teachers or lecturuers by their names instead of miss or sir. The interactions I've had at college has been positive 99% of the time anyways.

After my level 2 course I went on to do a level 3 course, and that was two years long. The first year was pretty fun as I made some good friends but the second year was well, it wasn't as fun due to the fact the classes got split up and whatnot
